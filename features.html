Every part of Susurr

Built for the way readers actually read.

Quiet tools that help you track your library, capture reflections, find your next read, and keep your reading life under your control.

01 — A quiet moment

Home & Quick Susurrate

Susurr opens with the book you are currently reading and gives you a calm place to continue your reading habit. When a thought hits, tap susurrate and capture it before it fades.

  • See your current read at a glance
  • Start a reflection from the home screen
  • A calm, focused space for your reading life
02 — Your shelves

Library Tracking

Keep a record of the books you own, the books you are reading, and the books waiting for the right evening. Organize your library by status, shelves, progress, and personal taste.

  • Track books by reading status
  • Create shelves for moods, genres, or collections
  • Follow your progress page by page
03 — In your own voice

Voice Reflections

Some thoughts are easier spoken than written. Record a susurr or write a note tied to the book you are reading. Whether spoken or written, your reflections stay connected to the moment you had them.

  • Record voice reflections while reading
  • Add written notes when you prefer typing
  • Attach reflections to books, pages, and ratings
04 — Your next read

AI Recommendations

When you are not sure what to read next, Susurr can help you search by feeling. Describe your mood, vibe, or craving, and get book recommendations that match the kind of reading moment you want.

  • Search for books by mood or vibe
  • Get thoughtful suggestions instead of endless scrolling
  • Use optional AI recommendations when you want them
05 — Yours to keep

Local Backups

Export your library as a ZIP backup with books, susurrs, audio, shelves, and covers. Import it later to restore your reading life on another device.

  • Export your library, susurrs, and audio
  • Import a backup to restore your data
  • Repair your library if whispers appear missing or miscategorized
06 — The foundation

Privacy First Design

Susurr is designed around a private reading experience. There are no public profiles, no social feed, and no pressure to turn your reading into content for anyone else.

  • No public profiles or social feeds
  • Your personal library stays under your control
  • Optional connected features are clearly separate

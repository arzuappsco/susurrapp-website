Every part of Susurr

Built for the way readers actually read.

Six quiet tools that work together to hold your reading life — and never ask you to hand it over.

01 — Your shelves

Library Tracking

Keep a record of the books you own and the books you've read. Mark what's in progress, what's finished, and what's waiting for the right evening.

  • Add books by title, author, and cover
  • Create custom shelves for moods, genres, or seasons
  • Track reading progress page by page
02 — In your own voice

Voice Reflections

Some thoughts are easier spoken than written. Record a reflection the moment you finish a chapter — a reaction, a memory, a line that stayed with you — and keep it tied to the book.

  • One-tap recording, stored locally
  • Attach reflections to a specific book or page
  • Revisit your voice as your reading evolves
03 — On the page

Reading Notes

Capture passages worth keeping and ideas worth chasing. Each note remembers where it came from, so your thoughts and the text stay together.

  • Write notes linked to book and page
  • Save quotes, questions, and reflections
  • Browse a journal of everything you've read
04 — Your next read

AI Recommendations

When you're ready for something new, Susurr offers suggestions drawn from your own shelves and reading history — thoughtful, not noisy. Recommendations are about your taste, never about selling you to anyone.

  • Suggestions based on what you've read
  • A short list, not an endless feed
  • Designed to respect your privacy
05 — Yours to keep

Local Backups

Because Susurr keeps nothing in the cloud, you stay in control of your own archive. Export your entire reading life to a single file, store it wherever you trust, and import it onto a new device whenever you need.

  • Export library, notes, and reflections in one file
  • Import to restore or move to a new phone
  • No servers, no middlemen — just your file
06 — The foundation

Privacy First Design

Privacy isn't a setting in Susurr — it's the architecture. There are no accounts to create and no servers to sync with. Everything you record lives on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere without you choosing to.

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no email required
  • Works fully offline
  • No analytics tracking your reading
